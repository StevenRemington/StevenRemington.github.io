# Steven Remington
**Systems Architect | Compiler Engineer | Physical Crafter**

I operate at the intersection of high-level logical abstractions and low-level physical constraints. My work is driven by the belief that software is not a neutral mechanism, but a manifestation of intent that must be architected with an intimate understanding of its host environment—whether that environment is a multi-node GPU cluster or a passively cooled microcontroller in a leather-bound cyberdeck.

Art and the act of creation is some of the human experiences to exist and it should be treated with love, care, and kindness.

I hold a Bachelor of Science in **Computer Science** with a minor in **Mathematics** from the **University of Florida**.

---

### The Digital Void: Compilers & Systems
My foundational expertise lies in **Compiler Infrastructure** and **Language Design**. I focus on:
*   **Parsing Strategies:** Developing high-signal, low-latency command structures for classic programming languages and agentic control.
*   **Memory Management:** Implementing deterministic resource allocation (custom allocators, pool management) for long-running edge daemons.
*   **C++20:** Leveraging modern systems programming to bridge the gap between abstract reasoning and silicon.

### The Physical Node: Edge AI & Autonomy
I architect **Agentic Systems** designed to survive outside the "infinite" compute of the cloud. This involves:
*   **Orchestration:** Managing local GGUF execution loops on Nvidia Jetson and Raspberry Pi hardware.
*   **Hardware/Software Co-design:** Building custom I/O modules and telemetry displays that expose the internal state of the "Cortex."
*   **Offline-First Philosophy:** Ensuring autonomy is physical, not dependent on continuous connectivity.

### The Artifact: Craft & Physical Manifestation
Design is not limited to code. I apply the same principles of modularity and structural integrity to physical fabrication:
*   **Textile Engineering:** A deep exploration of tactile systems through sewing, knitting, and quilting—treating fabric as a complex, multi-layered medium for structural patterns.
*   **Cosplay & Prop Design:** Utilizing EVA foam and leatherwork to build artifacts that bridge digital fiction and physical reality.
*   **Cyberdecks:** Engineering bespoke mobile computing nodes that house the hardware for my AI research.
*   **Acoustic Traditions:** A long-time musician currently focused on the **Octave Mandolin** and **Banjo**, specifically studying the rhythmic and melodic structures of Old-Time music.
